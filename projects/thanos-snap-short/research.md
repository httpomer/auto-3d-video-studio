# What if you were Thanos? — research notes (YouTube Short)

Researched 2026-11-25. Premise: the fictional "snap" removes a random half of all humans, each person independently
with probability ½. Everything below is either a sourced figure or exact arithmetic on that premise.

## Population
- World population, 1 July 2026: **≈ 8.30 billion** (UN World Population Prospects 2024, medium variant; 8,300,678,395).
  Live counters (Worldometer, ~8.33 B in Nov 2026) are extrapolations, so the UN figure is used.
  - https://population.un.org/wpp/ · https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/
- Half of 8.30 B = **4.15 billion** people.
- World population passed **4 billion in 1974** (UN-based dating; some sources say 1975). 4.15 B ≈ the world of the
  mid-1970s → narration says "the mid-nineteen-seventies".
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_population_milestones · https://www.britannica.com/topic/modernization/Population-change
- World population in 1950: ≈ 2.5 billion (UN). Used only as the first chart point.
- Growth rate: UN projects ≈ 68.9 million added in 2026 ≈ **0.83 % a year** (WPP 2024; Statistics Times gives 0.84 % for 2025).
  - https://m.statisticstimes.com/demographics/world-population.php
- Doubling time at 0.83 %/yr: ln 2 / ln 1.0083 = 0.6931 / 0.00827 ≈ **84 years** (assumes today's rate holds — stated as such).

## Air traffic
- Flightradar24, May 2023: **22,000 flights in the air at the same time**, the most it had ever tracked (all tracked aircraft).
  - https://mezha.ua/en/2023/05/29/flightradar24-recorded-a-record-number-of-planes-in-the-air/
  - https://www.flightradar24.com/blog/inside-flightradar24/the-busy-summer-skies-continuing-to-break-flight-tracking-records/
- Narration: "at peak times, more than twenty thousand aircraft are in the air at once".

## Probability (exact, from the premise)
- Two-pilot cockpit: P(both vanish) = ½ × ½ = **1 in 4**. The four equally likely outcomes: both stay, captain gone,
  co-pilot gone, both gone.
- Family of four: P(all four survive) = (½)⁴ = **1 in 16**.
