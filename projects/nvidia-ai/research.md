# Research — How NVIDIA is changing the AI industry

Researched 25 November 2026. Figures that move (market value, share estimates) are dated.

## History
- Founded 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem; the founding conversations took place at a Denny's near San Jose. (NVIDIA corporate timeline; Fortune)
- 1999: GeForce 256, marketed as the world's first GPU. (NVIDIA corporate timeline)
- 2006: CUDA released — lets developers use GPUs for general (non-graphics) computing. (NVIDIA corporate timeline)
- 2012: AlexNet (Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton) trained with CUDA on two NVIDIA GPUs wins ImageNet — the deep-learning breakthrough. A research project run on NVIDIA hardware, not an NVIDIA product.
- 2016: Huang personally delivers the first DGX-1 AI supercomputer to OpenAI. (Fortune, 22 Feb 2024)
- November 2022: ChatGPT launches; demand for AI compute explodes.

## Financials (NVIDIA press releases / SEC filings)
- Fiscal-year revenue: FY2023 $27.0B · FY2024 $60.9B · FY2025 $130.5B · FY2026 $215.9B (FY2026 ended 25 Jan 2026, +65 %).
- Q3 FY2027 (quarter ended 25 Oct 2026, reported 17 Nov 2026): revenue $115.8B (+103 % y/y, +20 % q/q);
  Data Center $107.6B (+110 % y/y) ≈ 93 % of revenue; GAAP gross margin 74.8 %; Q4 outlook $130.0B ± 3 %,
  assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China. Vera Rubin NVL72 has shipped.
  → One quarter now exceeds the whole of fiscal 2024 ($60.9B) and approaches all of fiscal 2025 ($130.5B).

## Market value
- Closed above $5 trillion on 24 April 2026 (CNBC); ~$5.5T on 13 May 2026 (Forbes); above $5.8T and "near $6T" on 6 Oct 2026 (Bloomberg).
- Late November 2026: roughly $3.9–4.0 trillion (Capital.com 23 Nov: ~$3.99T; CompaniesMarketCap: $3.977T).

## Leadership change (November 2026)
- NVIDIA announced that Jensen Huang stepped down as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately;
  CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a successor (company 8-K; TechCrunch, CNN, CNBC 12 Nov 2026).
- NVIDIA gave no reason. Media reports about the circumstances are unconfirmed allegations — NOT used in the video.
- Shares fell more than 10 % in pre-market trading on the news.

## Market position and competition
- Share of AI-accelerator revenue: estimates for 2026 range ~75–85 % (Silicon Analysts: 87 % in 2024 → 81 % in 2025 → ~75 % projected 2026). No audited figure exists; present as an estimate.
- AMD ≈ 5–8 %; hyperscaler custom chips (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, etc.) ≈ 12–18 % and growing faster
  (TrendForce: custom ASIC shipments +44.6 % y/y vs +16.1 % for merchant GPUs).
- CUDA: more than 11 million developers at its 20th anniversary in 2026 (NVIDIA's own figure, SC26).

## Customers and spending
- 2026 capital expenditure guidance: Amazon ≈ $220B, Alphabet $195–205B, Microsoft ≈ $170B, Meta $135–145B → about $730B combined, up from ≈ $410B in 2025.

## Product
- Vera Rubin NVL72 rack: ~3.6 exaFLOPS (NVFP4) vs ~1.1 for the previous Blackwell GB300 NVL72 rack; fully liquid cooled;
  roughly 156–227 kW per rack vs ~135 kW (integrator / analyst figures; NVIDIA has not published one official number).
